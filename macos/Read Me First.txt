Chalkboard Terminal for Mac
===========================

INSTALL
1. Drag Chalkboard Terminal into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. It opens in a Terminal window.

THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
- "Chalkboard cannot be opened" / "Apple could not verify": the app isn't from
  the App Store. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and
  click "Open Anyway" next to Chalkboard. (On older macOS, right-click the app
  and choose Open instead.) Or, in Terminal, run this once:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Chalkboard Terminal.app"
  Copying the app over a USB drive avoids this step entirely.
- "Chalkboard wants to control Terminal": click OK. That's how it opens its window.
- If your Mac doesn't have Python yet, Chalkboard offers to install Apple's free
  Command Line Developer Tools (which include Python). Let it finish, then open
  Chalkboard again.
- "Terminal would like to access files in your Documents folder": click OK.
  Exports are saved to Documents/Chalkboard.

KEYBOARD
- Esc goes back. On a Mac keyboard, Delete is backspace and Fn+Delete deletes
  forward. Control-A / Control-E jump to the start / end of a line.

WHERE THINGS LIVE
- Your lessons and assessments:
      ~/Library/Application Support/chalkboard/data.json
  To move your work from a Linux computer, copy its
  ~/.local/share/chalkboard/data.json to that spot (quit Chalkboard first).
- Exports: Documents/Chalkboard (changeable in Settings)

BOARD SLIDES (PNG FOR A CLASSROOM DISPLAY)
- In a lesson, press B, or choose BOARD SLIDE as the export format. The PNG is
  1920x1080 and is drawn with the Mac's built-in `sips` tool, so nothing extra
  needs to be installed.
- For school colors, open Settings and enter SCHOOL COLOR 1 (background) and
  SCHOOL COLOR 2 (headings) as HEX codes, like #7A0019 and #FFC72C.

FROM TERMINAL (OPTIONAL)
    python3 "/Applications/Chalkboard Terminal.app/Contents/Resources/chalkboard.pyz"
