Chalkboard for Mac
==================

INSTALL
1. Drag "Chalkboard" into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. Everything it needs is inside the app; there's nothing
   else to install. It runs on Intel and Apple silicon Macs.

THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
- "Chalkboard cannot be opened" / "Apple could not verify": the app
  isn't from the App Store. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
  down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Chalkboard. (On older macOS,
  right-click the app and choose Open instead.) Or, in Terminal, run this once:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Chalkboard.app"
- "Chalkboard would like to access files in your Documents folder":
  click OK. Exports are saved to Documents/Chalkboard.

USING IT
- Three looks: View > Modern, Bevel, or Pinstripe (also in Settings), each
  with a light and a dark mode.
- View > Switch to Terminal View (Command-Shift-W) shows the green-screen
  terminal view in the same window; press it again to come back.
- Back and Home are at the top of every window; Esc or Command-W also go back.
- Everything saves by itself.

WHERE THINGS LIVE
- Your lessons and assessments:
      ~/Library/Application Support/chalkboard/data.json
  Chalkboard Terminal (the terminal-only app) uses the same file, so you can switch
  between them any time.
- Exports: Documents/Chalkboard (changeable in Settings)
