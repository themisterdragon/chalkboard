**A test version of Chalkboard 3.1**: smoother, plus a school calendar, snow days, and at-home days.

**What's new**

- **Smooth scrolling everywhere.** Lessons, lists, and settings scroll all the way down again, follow a trackpad exactly, and glide with a mouse wheel. The whole window is quicker too.
- **Lesson Plans: filter by class, sort by anything.** Pick a class, then sort by the date it's taught, date changed, date made, title, or unit.
- **School calendar** (Settings): your first and last day, breaks, holidays, and teacher days, typed in or read from your district's calendar file (.ics). The Calendar and Today show them, and a lesson's date warns when it lands on one.
- **Shift lesson dates and Snow Day.** Move lessons later or earlier by school days, skipping weekends and days off. A preview first, a backup, and Edit > Undo Shift puts them back.
- **At-home days.** Set how many your district allows (and what it calls them), and keep a few at-home lessons ready for each unit: explained in writing, about half an hour, with questions. On the day, each class gets an unused one from the unit it's in, as a simple packet (PDF and Word) plus a plain-text copy to paste into Google Classroom. Then fill in the teacher log (how many took part, by technology or by paper, and who you talked to) and export it for the office.

**It doesn't touch your current Chalkboard.** The beta keeps its own copy of your lessons and assessments (copied from your regular Chalkboard the first time it opens) and exports to its own folder, so you can try it side by side.

## Download

| Your computer | Download | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Mac (macOS 11+, Apple silicon or Intel) | `Chalkboard-@VERSION@-macOS.dmg` | Open it, drag Chalkboard to Applications. First time: right-click Chalkboard > Open, then Open (on macOS 15+: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway) |
| Windows 10 / 11 | `Chalkboard-@VERSION@-windows.exe` | Double-click. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": More info > Run anyway |
| Linux (Intel/AMD) | `Chalkboard-@VERSION@-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | Unpack, double-click `chalkboard` (or run it in a terminal) |
| Linux (ARM, e.g. Raspberry Pi 4/5) | `Chalkboard-@VERSION@-linux-arm64.tar.gz` | The same |

The first time, a short setup offers to install Chalkboard (Applications, the Start menu, or your app menu).

This is a beta: please report anything odd. The Mac and Windows programs aren't signed with a paid developer certificate, so each asks once as above.
