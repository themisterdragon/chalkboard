CHALKBOARD FOR macOS (11 Big Sur or later; Apple silicon and Intel)

1. Drag Chalkboard into Applications (the folder in this window).
2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS may say it can't check Chalkboard for
   malicious software, because it isn't from the App Store. Right-click (or Control-click)
   Chalkboard, choose Open, then Open again. On macOS 15 and later: System Settings >
   Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.

Chalkboard works fully offline. Your lessons and assessments stay on this Mac.
